Important information & disclaimers

For planning purposes only. Results are estimates, not a mortgage approval, rate guarantee, lender quote, financial advice, or a commitment to lend. Confirm actual figures with your lender, mortgage broker, and legal professional before making a purchase decision.

  • Interest and payments: Calculations assume Canadian fixed-rate mortgages with nominal annual interest compounded semi-annually and simplified monthly or bi-weekly payment schedules. Lender rounding, payment dates, and renewal terms can change actual results.
  • Bi-weekly options: Accelerated bi-weekly payments equal half of the calculated monthly payment every two weeks (26 times a year), which generally shortens amortization. Regular bi-weekly is calculated separately.
  • CMHC / mortgage default insurance: Minimum down payment is 5% of the first $500,000 and 10% of the remainder below $1.5 million; at $1.5 million or above, at least 20% is required. Premiums use standard loan-to-value tiers, including a 0.20 percentage point surcharge for insured amortization over 25 years, and are illustrative. Eligibility, minimum down payment rules, property price limits, borrower qualifications, and applicable premium surcharges may affect your actual premium. Ontario's 8% provincial sales tax on the premium is estimated as an upfront cost, not added to the loan.
  • Renewal and payoff: The renewal illustration assumes the entered rate applies from year 4 onward, including subsequent renewals, and that your chosen extra payments continue. Actual future rates are unknown.
  • Prepayments: Extra payments may be limited by lender privileges; exceeding permitted amounts can trigger penalties. Check your mortgage agreement.
  • Monthly budget: The total includes only the fixed expenses you enter. It may exclude land transfer tax, legal fees, appraisal, inspections, moving costs, property taxes, condo fees, utilities, maintenance, and emergency reserves unless you include them yourself.

Accuracy: This tool does not account for every insured mortgage program, lender product, tax situation, or closing cost. Values are rounded for display and may differ slightly from lender statements.
